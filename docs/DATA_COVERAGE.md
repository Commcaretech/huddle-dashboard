# Reporting data coverage

The in-app Data coverage panel uses the [coverage catalogue](../shared/integration-coverage.js), the discovered GlowBBDashboard catalogue and released metrics in the latest published scope. Every report identifies its exact dates, source and centre. See [metric mapping and availability](GLOWBB_DATA_MAPPING.md).

GlowBBDashboard supplies data to KPI Performance, Participant Profiles, Health Data, Fitness and H.ART. All 13 reports retain manual reporting, implications and actions. Next Two Weeks and Next Three Months rely on staff-entered developments and planning.
